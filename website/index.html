FAQ

Questions sellers ask.

Short answers about calls, money and your Shopify orders. Anything else, write to us.

Does HaanJi cancel orders in Shopify by itself?

No. It adds a tag and a note, like cod-cancelled with the reason. You stay in control of what gets cancelled and what gets shipped.

What if the customer doesn’t pick up?

HaanJi tries again up to 3 times, 2 hours apart, only inside your calling hours. If there is still no answer, the order is tagged cod-no-answer. Unanswered calls are free.

What if the customer says “call me later”?

HaanJi asks when, and calls back at that time. If they say “raat 11 baje”, the call moves to 9 AM the next morning, because nobody is called outside your calling hours.

Can I change what HaanJi says?

Yes. Every line of the call is a plain text box in your dashboard, with a live preview. Change it, save it, and the next call uses it.

Do I need Shopify?

No. You can upload an Excel or CSV sheet of orders and download a sheet with the results. Shopify just makes it automatic.

What happens when my balance runs out?

Calls pause, they aren’t lost. You get a warning when the balance drops below ₹300. As soon as you recharge, every waiting customer is called.
